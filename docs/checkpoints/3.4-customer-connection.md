## 1. Approved requirements (founder brief "Mystery Hub 3.4")

### Three connected experiences, one coordinated system
- **Install Mystery Hub** has strategic priority when genuinely eligible.
- **Allow Notifications** applies to supported browser visitors, guests and members, not only installed-app users.
- **Follow the official WhatsApp Channel** (`https://whatsapp.com/channel/0029Vb97gokADTOKgN8Nzr0s`) is secondary. This is the approved new URL; `src/config/business.ts` keeps the old `0029VbBt1MGC6ZvlDJ8Biz3l` until Release B replaces it.

### Coordination rules
- Never two overlays at once.
- Nothing immediately after a dismissal.
- At most one unsolicited acquisition invitation per browsing session; explicit user actions are exempt.
- Operational messages come before promotions.
- Never over checkout, Paystack, auth, order recovery or critical order status, Admin, the Website Builder editor or Mystery Greetings.
- Voluntary entry points always remain.
- Each invitation keeps its own dismissal and consent state.

### Approved copy (refine only with a reason)
- **App:** "Mystery Hub, one tap away." / "Buy data, track orders and more, right from your home screen." / Install App · Not now. Use Add to Home Screen wording where required; no fake store buttons.
- **Notifications:** "Stay updated." / "Get order updates, service news and occasional offers." / Allow Notifications · Not now.
- **Channel:** "Stay in the Mystery. 💚" / "Follow our official channel for bundle updates, Friday Drops and special offers." / Follow Channel · Maybe later · Already following.

### Installation
- Preserve the existing system (`InstallAppPromo`, `useAppInstall`, `launchPolicy`, `installPrompt`, `appEnvironment`):
  - 30-day cooldown;
  - suppression after 3 dismissals;
  - published sites excluded.
- Distinguish prompt shown, accepted, confirmed installed (`appinstalled` or standalone), no longer offered, and opened standalone. Use a conservative pending-install state.
- `/app` page preserved.
- Engagement-based timing. A post-checkout moment is allowed only if the receipt stays usable.

### Channel invitation
- **Who:** guests and members.
- **Snooze and suppression:** Maybe later snoozes about 30 days; Already following suppresses in this browser; repeated dismissals reduce frequency.
- **Honesty:** a click is not a follow.
- **Storage:** local storage with safe failure; no database for guest popup state.
- **Links:** replace the old Channel URL everywhere first-party. Personal support chat stays support.

### Notifications
- **Flow:** one first-party invitation, then the browser permission, then server-confirmed registration, then one success state. No second "offers?" popup.
- **Consent:** keep browser permission, subscription, transactional preferences and promotional consent distinct.
  - Auditable, purpose-specific consent evidence.
  - Never infer marketing consent from browser permission.
  - Never re-enable someone who opted out.
  - Easy withdrawal.
  - Respect Ghana's data-protection rules on direct marketing.
- **Capability-based support:**
  - Android Chromium and installed PWA.
  - Desktop.
  - iOS/iPadOS Home Screen apps only. Safari tabs get concise Home Screen guidance.
  - Never offer what cannot work.
- **Guests:**
  - General announcements need voluntary consent.
  - Order-specific updates must be securely bound to the order and the browser capability. Never phone or reference alone.
  - Generic push payloads, with details only through authorized requests.
  - No fake users.
  - If it is not provably secure, gate it and say so.
- **Members:**
  - Permission only after a tap.
  - Session-bound subscriptions, server confirmation, and no leakage on logout or account switch.
  - Installed-app first-use invitation after the welcome sheet.
- **Inbox:** polish the existing authenticated notification centre. No invented history, no fake "read", no guest inbox.

### Admin Notification Center
- **Status and readiness:** truthful readiness (VAPID, paused or enabled, subscriptions, queue, failures, provider acceptance and its limits) and plain-language diagnostics with no secrets.
- **Composing:** composer with preview, validation, audience eligibility and explicit confirmation; templates (editable versus locked transactional, versioned, audited).
- **Audiences:** members, consenting marketing subscribers, guests (only with server-side consent), and devices versus people.
- **Sending:** idempotent and duplicate-safe, with pause, rate limits, audit and partial-failure reporting.
- **History** with filters.
- **Layout:** practical on laptop and phone.

### Backend and activation
- Extend the existing Web Push stack; no parallel system or external SaaS.
- Settings: `WEB_PUSH_ENABLED`, `WEB_PUSH_VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`, `WEB_PUSH_VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY`, `WEB_PUSH_VAPID_SUBJECT`, `WEB_PUSH_STORAGE_KEY`. Never expose or fake them.
- Merge with sending gated. Live delivery needs the operational gates, real-device verification and founder approval. No test sends to real customers, and no real payments to test push.

### Footer and contact (normal and December)
- Premium and compact.
- **Support:** WhatsApp 024 283 6323 (temporary, via PR #118), SMS 059 206 6298, Call 059 452 1108, email unchanged.
- **Community, kept separate:** WhatsApp Channel (new URL) and Telegram.
- **Preserve:** navigation, Help/FAQ, trust, legal, Staff Portal and seasonal compatibility.
- Never on customer-published sites.

### Copy and quality gates
- **Copy:** Apple-like minimalism (one heading, one sentence, one primary action, one dismissal), no jargon, no fake urgency.
- **Checks:** tsc, build, unit, integration, the full regression, native PostgreSQL for DB changes, real-browser UI and service-worker tests, accessibility, and widths 320/360/390/430/768/1280/1440 including short heights and safe areas.
- **Adversarial review:** cross-account, guest token replay, consent bypass, duplicate sends, subscription theft, stale sessions, misleading delivered or read states, and config leakage.

### Out of scope
- Contact Hub (#120), suppliers, AirtelTigo catalogue, Paystack execution, Wallet, Results Checker activation, December pricing, referral economics and the Greetings backend.
- Builder templates, Mystery AI and a native app.
- Notification SaaS, a second notification DB or event engine, and a site redesign.
