# Financial reconciliation V1 — evidence and release record

## Audit before implementation (November 2, 2026)

Fresh remote main: `ae964473a3d19801ade7842ffe849e12411faddb`. Isolated branch/worktree; PR #69 and Builder V7.2 work are excluded.

`CommercialService.initialize` and `WebsiteBusinessService.initialize` atomically create an order and an initialization operation, then set that operation to `pending` when Paystack initialization succeeds. Neither callback, webhook, dispatch nor terminal order reconciliation subsequently completes these checkout-operation rows. Their state describes initialization, not settlement. This is the current implemented model, with misleading reporting potential; it is not proof of 115 failed payments. No history rewrite or operation-state synchronization is included.

Paystack callback and signed webhook share exact saved reference/amount/currency validation (plus store identity metadata) before `FulfilmentService.processPaidOrder`. `OrdersStore.markOrderPaid` is idempotent and preserves supplier/terminal progress; late confirmation on a closed attempt raises review instead of blindly dispatching. Atomic dispatch claims and uncertain submission records prevent duplicate supplier POSTs. Closing a browser, losing a callback or duplicate notifications does not establish payment failure/delivery. This report never invokes these paths.

Wallet purchases commit their debit and order together. Original Wallet refunds require the exact debit and append one matching original-purchase credit; provider refunds are distinct. Top-ups credit once only after exact live payment identity checks, with verification timestamps. A pending top-up has no implied wallet credit. Restricted reversal/reward reviews remain separate from balances.

Store sale operations are separate seller liabilities, based on the immutable order's retail/wholesale/reserve/earning snapshot. `syncOrder` releases earnings after delivered status without review, reverses eligible terminal sales, or restricts unresolved reversals. Checkout amount is retail, sale operation amount is seller margin. Neither is platform net profit. Existing money movement and policy are unchanged.

Order resolution JSON and append-only history distinguish operator-attested manual service/external compensation from verified original-method refunds. Manual closure preserves the original failed/cancelled/refund_pending status and does not manufacture a supplier delivery timestamp. Supplier reimbursement never establishes customer refund completion. A processed Paystack refund requires exact linked transaction/reference/amount/currency and completion evidence; pending/processing does not close it.

Initial aggregate production SELECTs confirmed 88 pending direct and 27 pending store checkout operations: 83 linked paid/delivered, 29 closed without recorded payment, and 3 paid failed/cancelled orders. All three stopped paid cases already had resolved manual-fulfillment records. Across all orders, all seven historical paid stopped cases (including the one refund_pending status) had structured manual fulfillment and matching resolved history. They must not be reopened as unresolved remedies. Three of the 83 delivered checkout orders lacked delivered_at; status alone must not invent that timestamp. Later SELECT observed 590 orders and 41 ledger rows, seven credited top-ups with matching credits/verification and five unconfirmed pending top-ups (earlier four); concurrent production activity makes these observations time-sensitive. No private customer data or raw provider responses were retrieved.

## Read-only model

One record per order, independent of callback/webhook/publication event counts. Additional records cover top-ups, reward reviews and finance operations whose required order link is absent. Archived order history remains available. Registered synthetic fixtures are excluded from default counters but may be inspected by exact reference as non-payment test history. Complimentary/operator-recorded activity is not a verified sale.

- **Completed:** stored payment success + paid timestamp + valid GHS amount/reference, consistent finance/snapshot evidence, delivered status and delivery timestamp, no unresolved review/remedy. This is stored application evidence, not a fresh Paystack/supplier inquiry.
- **Awaiting confirmation:** no payment/ledger confirmation and no contradictory supplier/delivery evidence. Age alone does not prove payment collection or financial loss.
- **Closed without confirmed payment:** closed attempt without payment/acceptance/delivery evidence. Late confirmations can still arrive; the report does not certify an external account was never charged.
- **Monitoring:** recorded paid progress, pending customer refund with matching nonterminal verification evidence, or durable earnings reconciliation in progress. A supplier refund_pending flag alone is not customer refund progress.
- **Needs review:** contradictory identities/amounts/currencies or ledger evidence, missing required order links, unresolved paid stopped/uncertain submissions, unbacked available seller credits, unresolved reversals, or MTN data progress beyond the documented exceptional 48-hour window. Time-based review is a recommendation, never a failure/refund/debt finding; no universal SLA is invented for other services.
- **Evidence unavailable:** incomplete payment/delivery/resolution evidence. Never silently promoted to completed/refunded.
- **Resolved/recorded:** separately labelled structured operator attestation, matching recorded original-method refund, verified Wallet funding credit or recorded funding reversal. Manual service is not supplier delivery; external compensation is not provider-verified refund. A resolved historical failure stays historical.
- **Not a verified purchase:** complimentary/operator-recorded or registered synthetic activity, explicitly separated from confirmed payment counts.

Contradictory financial facts take precedence over apparently resolved JSON. Exact original Wallet debits/credits and seller ledger entries must match their own operation/user and snapshots; no current pricing or policy is substituted. Resolution closure requires matching version/outcome history and complete evidence, without returning private notes. Pending initialization operation states never overrule linked order/payment/remedy facts.

## Implementation constraints and verification plan

On-demand Admin Finance reporting only, using one shared-pool connection and a repeatable-read READ ONLY transaction, local statement/lock deadlines, parameterized exact-reference lookup, bounded pagination and a narrow response projection. No worker, provider calls, customer contact joins, schema/RLS/grant change, mutation or financial service reuse. Inspect native and production read-only query plans before release; report future index needs separately rather than running production DDL.

Preserve all existing Finance tabs and actions; add a read-only reconciliation tab and navigation into the existing Orders/Finance workflows. Avoid edits to Builder/editor/template/analytics presentation files. Baseline regression: 2,822 passed, zero failed, 37 skipped. Final verification/review/deployment results will be recorded in the PR and release report. Native PostgreSQL and synthetic browser tests must execute separately from optional full-suite skips.

Rollback: revert this application-only reporting PR through normal GitHub/Render deployment. No database rollback, historical rewrite or security relaxation is needed. Any confirmed money-movement defect is a separate approval task.

## Implemented reporting and evidence rules

New files: `shared/financeReconciliation.ts`, `server/db/financeReconciliationStore.ts`, `server/routes/financeReconciliationApi.ts`, `src/services/financeReconciliationApi.ts` and `src/components/admin/sections/AdminFinanceReconciliation.tsx`. Existing changes are limited to mounting the GET route in `financeApi.ts`, adding the Finance tab and lazy loading in `AdminFinanceSection.tsx`, and adding the native reporting test to the existing database-security CI. Supporting files are the API/native tests, synthetic browser fixture and `scripts/reviewFinanceReconciliation.py`.

`GET /api/admin/finance/reconciliation` retains the existing Finance Admin guard and adds no new role or authority. Its input is an allowlisted group filter, exact public order/internal order/supported finance-operation reference, and integer pagination (default 25, maximum 50, offset at most 100000). Linked checkout/purchase/sale/refund operation IDs resolve to their one canonical order. Top-up/review/orphan IDs resolve to their own evidence record. It does not search customer contact details or expose raw payment references. All responses, including unauthorized errors, are no-store. The new route is rate limited to 30 on-demand reads/minute/IP under the existing limiter convention.

One shared-pool connection executes one aggregate SELECT inside a repeatable-read READ ONLY transaction with local 2500ms statement and 500ms lock deadlines; it releases on success/failure. Counters and page rows are from the same snapshot. There is no cache, background polling or worker. The all-time aggregate is linear in history size; measured current plans are acceptable. If growth requires new indexes, that is a separately approved schema task. Timeout/unavailable evidence returns 503, never fabricated zero counts or partial financial success.

The report-only tab does not load the existing withdrawal/control/operation screens. Those resume their normal reads when opened. Existing Summary, Requests, customer finance history, liabilities, settings, confirmations and mutation endpoints are preserved. The new UI has no payment/refund/settlement button; order links reuse the existing hash-based drawer route and funding/review links open existing customer finance history. Exact lookups reset the list view to All records. Snapshot scope, per-row reasons, original states, archive status and relevant dates are visible. Counters are informational, matching the existing Finance summary component language.

Additional consistency checks prevent misleading success: original-method Wallet debit/refund count/identity agreement; no money attached to initialization rows; unsupported/simulated initialization evidence; seller credit/reversal checks including zero-margin and missing metadata; exact compensating operation counts; complete resolution JSON and actor agreement with append-only history; frozen snapshot arithmetic accepting equivalent integral JSON number representations; and Marketplace refund/cancelled states independently of its global order status. No current retail/reserve/reward policy is substituted.

## Read-only production evidence and limits

The production EXPLAIN of the report SELECT completed with 13.237ms planning / 32.482ms execution, 847 shared buffer hits and no physical reads. No index or schema change was made. A later aggregate snapshot classified 369 orders as payment/delivery recorded, 182 closed without recorded payment, 4 awaiting confirmation, 2 progressing, 18 non-purchase, 7 operator-attested historical services, 8 verified funding credits, 12 missing delivery timestamps and one delivered order retaining an under_review resolution. These are observation-time counts, not hardcoded UI values. The latter 13 records require evidence inspection; they do not establish debts or losses. The historical refund_pending/manual-service record remains resolved. No provider verification or production financial mutation was performed.

Missing timestamps and an open review alongside recorded delivery should be investigated through existing authorized history. Do not backfill delivery times, automatically close reviews, repeat refunds or change balances to make counters green. No money-movement defect or customer debt has been proven by these reporting observations.
